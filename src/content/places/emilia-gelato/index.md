I expanded Emilia’s concept far beyond traditional gelato, creating a line of oversized, soft-baked artisanal cookies in signature flavors and a premium crêpe menu with custom sauces, decadent toppings and gelato pairings.

I also developed new gelato flavors that honor Italian craftsmanship while appealing to both local Panamanian guests and the Jewish kosher community — then engineered an outdoor catering setup for private events, trained the team, and left behind complete, standardized recipe books.
