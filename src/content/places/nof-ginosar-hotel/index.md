One intensive month, working from morning to night across the peak High Holidays of Rosh Hashanah and Sukkot. I restructured the hotel’s entire pastry department, introduced modern production techniques and standardized a new portfolio of recipes.

Alongside the Executive Chef and the F&B leadership, I trained the pastry team hands-on and designed scalable, premium menus for high-occupancy family dining, buffets, conferences and intimate banquets — from elevated everyday cakes to signature artisanal cookies, all to the highest kosher standards.
