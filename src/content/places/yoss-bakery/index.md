Yoss Bakery + Café brought me from Israel to Panama City to help build a kosher bakery unlike anything else in the city.

Together we created the menu from the ground up — laminated croissants and pain au chocolat, babkas, challah, cakes and cookies — and got the bakery ready to welcome its first customers.
