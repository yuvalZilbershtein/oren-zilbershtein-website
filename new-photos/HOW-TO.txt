ADDING PHOTOS
=============

1. Inside this folder, make one folder per place, named in English letters:

     new-photos/
       Hotel Name/        ← all the photos from that hotel
       Some Bakery/
       chef/              ← portraits of the chef (optional)

2. Copy the photos and videos in (straight from the phone is fine — iPhone
   HEIC and .mov too). Add the business's logo, named "logo.png" or "logo.jpg".

3. In a terminal, in the chef-website folder, run:

     npm run add-photos

   Each place gets its own gallery page. Photos are resized for the web,
   hidden location data is removed, and videos get a cover picture.

4. For a new place, open src/content/places/<place>/index.md and fill in
   the city, country, year and a short description.

Chef photos: in the chef/ folder, name the main portrait "hero.jpg" and the
second one "about.jpg" before running the command.
